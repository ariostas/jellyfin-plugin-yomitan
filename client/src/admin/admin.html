Yomitan

Dictionaries for looking up words in Japanese subtitles. They are shared by everyone on this server, and each user chooses which to use, in what order, from Settings, Yomitan. A dictionary is a zip file in Yomitan's format.

Lookups are off for each user until they turn them on: with the 文A button next to the subtitle button in the player, during Japanese subtitles, or under their picture, Settings, Yomitan. The first time they play Japanese subtitles, a notice tells them so.

Add a dictionary

None come with the plugin. Yomitan's list of dictionaries says where to get them: JMdict or Jitendex for words, JMnedict for names, KANJIDIC for kanji, and frequency and pitch dictionaries on top.
